Our Mission

The Data & Media Laboratory (DM Lab) was established in March 2020 under the Department of Informatics and Telecommunications at the University of Peloponnese. Our mission encompasses three core objectives:

  • check_circle Conduct high-level theoretical and applied research in data science and artificial intelligence
  • check_circle Support undergraduate and postgraduate studies with hands-on research experience
  • check_circle Foster collaboration with researchers, academic institutions, industry, and government organizations

Facilities & Resources

The DM Lab is equipped with modern computational infrastructure, including high-performance computing clusters, GPU servers for deep learning, and distributed data processing systems. Our researchers have access to extensive datasets, software licenses, and cloud computing resources.

Located within the University of Peloponnese campus, the lab provides a collaborative workspace designed to facilitate innovation, with dedicated areas for team meetings, seminars, and student projects.
